# Emergency Fund — governance policy (DRAFT)

A starting draft for The Dollar Chain's committee/trustees to adopt and adapt.
This is **not legal advice** — have your association's rules and a lawyer/accountant
sanity-check it before you go live with real grants. Square brackets `[ ]` are
decisions for you to make.

---

## 1. Purpose
The Emergency Fund is a coast-wide pot for **genuine, can't-wait crises** affecting
people in our community — where waiting for the normal weekly member vote would
cause real harm.

## 2. How it's funded
- **10% of every donation, after running costs**, is transferred to the Emergency
  Fund (the rest funds the donor's suburb causes; 10% of total covers operating costs).
- The fund accrues automatically; it is not subject to the weekly vote.
- A target floor/ceiling may be set, e.g. keep a minimum buffer of `[$____]` and,
  if the fund exceeds `[$____]`, the surplus rolls into the general local pots.

## 3. What it can fund (eligibility)
Genuine emergencies for someone living in / connected to the southern Gold Coast,
such as: escaping domestic & family violence; a single parent in sudden crisis;
natural-disaster relief (fire/flood/storm); a safe night indoors; urgent medical or
medication costs; sudden loss of income; preventing imminent eviction or utility
disconnection; funeral/bereavement help. Not an exhaustive list — use judgement.

## 4. What it will NOT fund
- Anything illegal, or that could enable harm (e.g. handing cash to a perpetrator).
- Ongoing/indefinite living costs (it's a bridge, not a salary).
- Political donations, or anything benefiting a committee member personally (see §8).
- Requests that belong with a statutory service (we refer, we don't replace 000 / crisis lines).

## 5. Who approves, and how fast
- **Small grants up to `[$250]`:** any `[one]` authorised committee member may approve
  on their own, aiming for **same-day**.
- **Grants `[$250]`–`[$1,000]`:** require **`[two]`** committee members to agree.
- **Grants over `[$1,000]`:** require **`[the full committee / a quorum of ___]`**.
- Target turnaround for urgent requests: **`[within 24–48 hours]`**.
- Pay suppliers/services **directly** where possible (motel, removalist, pharmacy,
  utility) rather than cash, both for safety and clean records.

## 6. Verification (light but real)
- A brief written note of: who it's for (initials/ref, not full public name), the
  situation, the amount, and how it was paid.
- Where practical, confirm need via a **referring service** (DV service, school,
  GP, community org) — this protects both the recipient and the fund.
- No grant to the same individual more than `[twice in 12 months]` without full
  committee sign-off.

## 7. Privacy & safety (critical — especially DV)
- **Never publish identifying details.** Public logs show amount + category + date only.
- Store any personal details securely, share on a strict need-to-know basis, and
  **delete** them once the grant is closed and reconciled.
- For domestic/family-violence cases, follow the lead of the specialist service and
  never do anything that could reveal a person's location or situation.

## 8. Conflict of interest
- A committee member must **declare** any personal connection to a request and **not**
  take part in approving it.
- Grants to committee members, their family or close associates require independent
  sign-off by the rest of the committee and must be recorded.

## 9. Records & transparency
- Every grant is logged on the public **Transparency** page (amount, category, date —
  never identifying details).
- Keep receipts/invoices on file for audit.
- Report the Emergency Fund balance and total granted in each monthly report.

## 10. Review
- Review this policy and the caps **`[every 6 months]`**, or after any incident.
